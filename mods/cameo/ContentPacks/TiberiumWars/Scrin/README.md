# Scrin content pack

This is a structures-only foundation for the future Scrin faction. It is not a
playable roster yet, and the faction remains hidden from the lobby. In
particular, the Drone Platform must eventually be deployed from a Drone Ship;
the platform is not registered as a starting actor.

The nine structure sheets contain one body frame followed by one indexed ground
shadow frame. Their magenta regions use Cameo's dynamic `player_rgba` palette.
The actors currently expose only the shared static-building behavior needed by
maps and the editor. Production roles, costs, power, prerequisites, construction
animations, destruction art, AI, and balance are intentionally deferred.

The sprites were independently extracted and rendered from the official
*Command & Conquer 3: Kane's Wrath* assets. The source models and textures are
copyright Electronic Arts and remain the property of their respective owner.
